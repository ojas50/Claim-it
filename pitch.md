# Claim-It — 5-Bullet Pitch

1. **Problem** — Health and accident insurance claims in India get delayed or rejected because of missing documents, unclear policy clauses, sub-limits, and messy paperwork. Most policyholders are more comfortable speaking in regional languages than filling English forms.

2. **Solution** — Claim-It is a voice-first insurance claim copilot. The user narrates their story in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali or English, uploads photos of their policy and bills (including handwritten ones), and gets a line-by-line coverage check with exact clause citations, gap detection, an editable claim letter, and a spoken summary — all in one mobile-first wizard.

3. **Why voice + vision** — **Saaras** captures the narrative as it happened, **Sarvam Vision** reads crumpled/handwritten documents that generic OCR misses, and **Sarvam-105B** reasons against the actual clauses to produce defensible verdicts (covered/partly/not/unclear) with confidence scores. **Bulbul** closes the loop by speaking the answer back in the same language.

4. **Impact** — For a typical ₹1.5 lakh claim, Claim-It surfaces that only ~₹1.21 lakh may be payable, shows a ₹33k patient share, and flags exactly what to collect (discharge summary, pre-authorisation, prescription, ID proof) before submission. That prevents avoidable rejections, cuts TAT for first-pass review, and makes claims accessible to first-time policyholders who don’t know insurance jargon.

5. **What's next** — **WhatsApp integration** (share photos + voice in one chat), **insurer/TPA APIs** for pre-auth and claim tracking, **hospital network lookup** (cashless vs reimbursement), **regional language expansion**, and an **audit trail** of all human edits to build trust with customers and partners. The same Sarvam modules plug straight in — we built around them from day one.
